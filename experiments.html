MathML extra credit

Einstein's mass-energy equivalence relates energy E, mass m, and the speed of light c.

E=mc2

Why practical use took longer

Being part of a specification did not mean that all browsers implemented MathML consistently. Firefox and Safari supported it earlier, while Chrome and Chromium-based Edge lacked enabled support through version 108. Their support from version 109 removed a major cross-browser obstacle. MathML Core also gives browser implementations a more precisely defined subset. MDN dates broad cross-browser availability to January 2023.

Sources: Can I use MathML support table and MDN MathML documentation.
